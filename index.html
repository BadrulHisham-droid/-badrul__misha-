SolatGuide

Prayer times · Qibla · Tasbih

SolatGuide is a simple Islamic companion for your day. See accurate prayer times for your location, find the Qibla direction, and keep count of your dhikr.

The problem it solves

Prayer apps are often crowded with ads and menus. SolatGuide keeps the essentials one tap away, so you always know the next prayer and which way to face.
